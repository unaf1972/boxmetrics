How Box Fill Works

This calculator divides the box dimensions by the product dimensions to estimate how many items fit in a single orientation. Real-world packing may allow more or fewer items depending on product shape and packing method.

The Formula

Items per Box = floor(L ÷ pL) × floor(W ÷ pW) × floor(H ÷ pH)

Void fill space is calculated as box volume minus total product volume.

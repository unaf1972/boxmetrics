How Dimensional Weight Works

Dimensional weight (DIM weight) is a pricing technique used by shipping carriers. It reflects the amount of space a package occupies in relation to its actual weight. Carriers charge based on the greater of the two.

The Formula

DIM Weight = (Length × Width × Height) ÷ DIM Divisor

Frequently Asked Questions

What is billable weight?

Billable weight is the weight the carrier uses to calculate your shipping cost. It's the greater of actual weight or dimensional weight, rounded up.

Does USPS use dimensional weight?

USPS uses DIM weight for packages larger than 1 cubic foot (1,728 cubic inches). The DIM divisor is 166.

How can I reduce my DIM weight?

Use smaller boxes, eliminate excess void fill, and choose packaging that fits your product tightly.

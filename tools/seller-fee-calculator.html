Frequently Asked Questions

What are Etsy's fees in 2026?

Etsy charges a 6.5% transaction fee on the total order (including shipping), plus a payment processing fee of roughly 3% + $0.25. There's also a $0.20 listing fee per item.

Does Shopify charge a transaction fee?

Shopify doesn't charge a per-sale transaction fee if you use Shopify Payments. If you use a third-party gateway, they charge an additional 0.5%–2%.

How do I know if I'm profitable?

A healthy profit margin for e-commerce is 15–30%. Below 10% is risky — a single return or ad spike can wipe out your profit.

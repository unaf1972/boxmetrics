How Pallet Loading Works

This calculator estimates how many cartons fit on a pallet based on carton dimensions, pallet size, and maximum load height. It tries both orientations (length-wise and width-wise) and picks the one that fits the most cartons per layer.

The Formulas

Cartons per Layer = max( orientation A, orientation B )

Where orientation A uses carton length along pallet length, and orientation B uses carton length along pallet width.

Layers per Pallet = floor( Max Load Height ÷ Carton Height )

Total Cartons = Cartons per Layer × Layers per Pallet

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a standard pallet size?

The most common pallet in North America is 48" × 40" (GMA pallet). Euro pallets are 47" × 39", and square pallets are 48" × 48".

How high can I stack a pallet?

Standard truck height allows up to 110" total including the pallet (usually 5-6" tall). For warehouse storage, 60" of load height is common, but check your carrier's limits.

Why does orientation matter?

Rotating cartons can significantly change how many fit per layer. Always test both orientations in your layout.
